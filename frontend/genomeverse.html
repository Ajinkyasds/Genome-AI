EXHIBITION SCIENTIFIC GUIDE

The 3 Big Ideas of DNA

• 01 — Structure (Double Helix): DNA has two outer handrails made of sugar and phosphate, wound around each other in an antiparallel corkscrew.

• 02 — The 4-Letter Code (Base Pairing): The steps inside are matching pairs: Adenine (A) always pairs with Thymine (T) via 2 hydrogen bonds; Guanine (G) always pairs with Cytosine (C) via 3 hydrogen bonds.

• 03 — Separation (The Zipper): Hydrogen bonds act like a molecular zipper. They hold the strands securely, but when the cell needs to copy or read the manual, an enzyme called Helicase unzips them cleanly.
